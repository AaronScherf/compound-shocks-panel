## Checks done

- DTM P-codes and names match COD-AB v03 exactly. The state codes in the Gemini note (SD01 Northern and so on) are
  wrong; COD-AB v03 uses SD01 Khartoum.
- WFP market names map to COD-AB localities through 14 aliases found by placing each market's coordinates inside
  the polygons (5,133 rows renamed).
- DTM is a stock: 19,666 of 30,869 in-period rows were superseded by a later report in the same month or by a
  preferred DTM series; reason rows are summed.

## Price gaps and conflict (descriptive, no test)

Localities that ever have a sorghum price have more events per month than localities that never do (5.81 vs 1.67;
per 100,000 residents 2.17 vs 0.94). Within market localities, months without a price have more events than months
with one (8.47 vs 4.62), a pattern that appears from 2023 (2021: 2.66 with a price vs 1.57 without; 2023: 5.57 vs
12.19). Differences may reflect where markets are placed. The 18 localities with prices hold 17% of the population.

## Known limits

- Population is a single 2024 estimate repeated across months; Abyei has none.
