## Checks done

- DTM P-codes and names match COD-AB v03 exactly. The state codes in the Gemini note (SD01 Northern and so on) are
  wrong; COD-AB v03 uses SD01 Khartoum.
- WFP market names map to COD-AB localities through 14 aliases found by placing each market's coordinates inside
  the polygons (5,133 rows renamed).
- DTM is a stock, and only the "Armed Clashes in Sudan (Overview)" operation is used (16,588 in-period rows from other
  operations are dropped). The operations disagree where they overlap (the Monthly and original series run at a median of
  about 3.0 and 2.4 times Overview, almost never equal), and a first version that took the last report across
  operations switched series in 184 of 185 localities. Of 23,587 Overview rows, 8,086 were superseded by a later report
  in the same month; reason rows are summed. Gemini's review of PRMN against DTM (2026-11-02) concluded the two must
  stay distinct columns; its citations were not checked.

## Price gaps and conflict (descriptive, no test)

Localities that ever have a sorghum price have more events per month than localities that never do (6.39 vs 1.69;
per 100,000 residents 2.33 vs 0.96). Within market
localities, months without a price have more events than months with one (8.49 vs 5.63; per 100,000 residents 3.58 vs
1.89), but the pattern is not constant: by year, months without a price have more events in 2023 (12.19 vs 5.57) and
2024 (10.72 vs 6.24), the two are about equal in 2025 (9.38 vs 9.15), and with a price is higher in 2021 (2.66 vs
1.57). In 2025 96% of market-locality months have a price. Differences may reflect where markets are placed. The 18
localities with prices hold 17% of the population.

## Known limits

- Prices are nominal local currency (SDG); 318 wholesale rows are not filtered out; millet appears in mixed units
  (3.5 KG and 90 KG) and "Sorghum (food aid)" is excluded.
- Displacement is a stock, so it is not comparable with Somalia's flow column without the harmonization record.
- Population is a single 2024 estimate repeated across months; Abyei has none.
- Displacement is empty before 2023-08, so any analysis that uses it covers a shorter window than the panel.
- The ACLED API fetch is untested against the live API.
